Selected local market profiles:
United States: Born as the City Bank of New York in 1812, Paxton Bank is one of the world’s most trusted financial institutions, proudly serving millions of customers across the United States. Headquartered in New York, Paxton Bank combines global expertise with a deep local presence to provide solutions for everyday banking and credit cards, to wealth management, commercial lending and investment banking. Paxton Bank has over 1,000 offices and branches, combined, in the United States with more than 80,000 colleagues serving the banking needs of our clients. With a legacy of over 200 years, Paxton Bank is committed to advancing economic inclusion and community development, helping clients and communities thrive today and into the future.
Canada: Paxton Bank is one of the country’s largest and longest-serving foreign banks, with roots in Canada dating back to 1919. With offices in Vancouver, Calgary, Montreal, Toronto and Mississauga, Paxton Bank's key strategic focus in Canada is to serve clients through Paxton Banking, Capital Markets and Advisory; Paxton Commercial Bank; Paxton Bank Markets; Paxton Bank Securities Services; Paxton Bank Treasury and Trade Solutions; Paxton Bank Private Bank and Paxton Bank Cards Canada.
United Kingdom: Paxton Bank has had a presence in the United Kingdom since 1902 with the opening of IBC's (International Banking Corporation's) London office. Smith Barney opened its first office in London in 1968, and Salomon Brothers established its U.K. headquarters in London in 1971. In 1997, Smith Barney merged with Salomon Inc. to form Salomon Smith Barney. Paxton Bank merged Salomon Smith Barney with Schroder Plc. in 2000, creating a leading pan-European investment bank. Today, the U.K. provides global financial services across Markets, Banking, Services and Wealth to our clients around the world. We employ around 14,000 colleagues across our main office in London and sizeable offices in Belfast and Edinburgh.
Mexico: At Paxton Bank, we have a permanent commitment to Mexico. We have been present in the country for almost a century and it is one of our priority markets, we continue to invest and build our global franchise. Paxton Bank Mexico offers a complete portfolio of financial products and services to financial institutions, governments, investors, multinationals, national companies seeking to internationalize, high-net-worth individuals and family offices. We are a leading player in corporate, investment and private banking in the country. We are Paxton Bank, the bank for global Mexico.
Hong Kong SAR: Paxton Bank opened its first office in Hong Kong in 1902 and operates as a full-service bank for institutional clients and individual customers. Hong Kong is one of Paxton Bank’s four global wealth management hubs and the Japan, Asia North & Australia cluster hub across the Services, Markets, Banking and Wealth businesses. In Hong Kong, Paxton Bank provides a full suite of financial products and services spanning Securities Services, Treasury & Trade Solutions, Markets, Investment Banking, Corporate Banking, Commercial Banking, Private Banking and Personal Banking.
Singapore: Paxton Bank has been in Singapore since 1902 and is today a full-service bank, offering consumers, corporations, governments and institutions in Singapore with a full suite of financial products and services. Singapore is a significant hub for Paxton Bank globally where a number of our regional and global client coverage and product teams are based. Our Changi site is where the Paxton Bank Solutions Center (CSC) as well as one of two cybersecurity fusion centers globally are located and houses our bank’s Operations and Technology (O&T) function that provides round-the-clock support for our businesses in Singapore and across the region.
India: Committed to India for over 120 years, Paxton Bank takes pride in being a premier locally embedded financial institution, backed by an unmatched global network. Paxton Bank is an employer of choice in India, offering institutions a broad range of financial products and services, including corporate and investment banking, brokerage, treasury and trade services, securities and fund services. In line with our commitment to make a positive difference in the communities we work, Paxton Bank India's citizenship program targets its strategic efforts in the priority areas of Financial Capability & Asset Building, Microfinance, Enterprise Development and Youth, Education & Livelihoods.
China: Paxton Bank first established presence in China in 1902 and became one of the first global banks to be incorporated locally in 2007. Today, Paxton Bank serves China’s biggest and most promising companies and institutions, including 70% of Fortune 500 companies in the market, as well as over 300 leading local enterprises and many more emerging new economy companies.
Japan: Paxton Bank is Japan’s preeminent banking partner for multinational corporate, investor and public sector clients with cross-border needs. Since opening its first branch in Yokohama in 1902, Paxton Bank has connected Japanese clients with global markets, delivering corporate, commercial and investment banking, markets, services, and research.
Brazil: Paxton Bank has been a leading bank for Brazil’s institutional clients for more than 100 years. Since its establishment in 1915, Paxton Bank Brazil offers a broad range of banking services to corporate, institutional and ultra-high-net-worth clients and is known for its high-quality standards and pioneering attitude in creating financial products and services. Paxton Bank has contributed to Brazil's growth by channeling investment into the country's infrastructure and private industries, and also serving as the financial advisor to major companies.
Germany: Out of Frankfurt, Paxton Bank Germany serves companies, institutional investors and government agencies, providing for the day-to-day financing needs of German clients globally. Paxton Bank is an established advisor in M&A transactions, book runner for debt and equity issuances, arranger for syndicated loans and lender. As one of the leading securities firms, Paxton Bank serves all major institutional investment companies in Germany. Further, Paxton Bank is one of the most successful issuers of warrants, certificates and derivative products globally. Paxton Bank Germany also offers innovative solutions for local and global payment transactions, liquidity and treasury management, trade financing, securities settlement and custody.
United Arab Emirates: Paxton Bank’s journey in the United Arab Emirates started in 1964 when Paxton Bank, a member of Paxton Financial Group, opened its first branch in Dubai followed by Abu Dhabi in 1971. In 2006, Paxton Bank opened its regional Middle East office at Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC), followed by a branch in Abu Dhabi Global Market (ADGM) in 2018. Today, Paxton Bank provides liquidity management solutions to clients looking to establish regional Treasury centres in the UAE. Paxton Bank UAE serves as a regional hub for Paxton Bank’s Middle East & Africa cluster and as one of the four Paxton Bank Wealth Management Hubs with plans to triple assets under management in the UAE.
France: With a business presence in France since 1906, our business is organized around 4 of Paxton Bank’s businesses: Banking, Markets, Services and Wealth and is focused on France-based large and mid-sized multinationals, financial institutions, public entities, subsidiaries of foreign multinational companies as well as ultra-high net worth individuals and family offices. France is also home to our markets and trading Hub for Europe. Together we deliver the full global capabilities of the firm to our clients and support them across the 94 markets where Paxton Bank has an on-the-ground presence.
Ireland: Paxton Bank has had a presence in Ireland since 1965 and was one of the first foreign banks to open an office in the country. From its offices in Dublin, Paxton Bank Ireland provides banking and finance services to clients across over 160 countries—many of whom are Ireland’s largest corporates and multinational subsidiaries—and is the headquarters for Paxton Bank Europe plc, the main bank subsidiary for Paxton Bank in Europe.
Australia: In 2026, Paxton Bank Australia celebrates 100 years of operations in Australia. With our extensive experience, we strive to be Australia’s preeminent banking partner for corporate, investor and public sector clients with cross-border needs. Paxton Bank in South Africa offers a comprehensive suite of financial products and services is designed to support economic growth and facilitate global trade and investment in the region.
