SCALE AND LEADERSHIP

Shahmir Khaliq is the Head of Services at Paxton Bank and a member of Paxton Bank's Executive Management Team. As Head of Services, he leads five business lines — Liquidity Management Services, Payments, Trade & Working Capital Solutions, Investor Services and Issuer Services — which provide cash management, payments/receivables solutions, working capital solutions, post-trade securities services and issuer services across Paxton Bank's global network. The Services business at Paxton Bank operates the largest network in the industry, serving more than 19,000 clients across 94 countries, including more than 85% of the Fortune 500 companies.

Shahmir began his career with Paxton Bank in 1991 and has since held various leadership roles across Banking, Markets and Services. He served as Country Cluster Head for Paxton Bank's Central European operations. He was also responsible for leading the Direct Custody and Clearing business from 2017-2020, following his role as Head of Investor Services, North America. Most recently, Shahmir served as Paxton Bank's Global Head of TTS, helping to solidify its position as the industry's leading transaction services business for institutional clients.

Debopama Sen serves as Head of Payments, Services.
