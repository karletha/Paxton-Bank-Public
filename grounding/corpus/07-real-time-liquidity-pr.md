For Immediate Release Paxton Financial Group Inc. (NYSE: C)

Paxton Bank Drives Liquidity Innovation with the Launch of Real-Time Liquidity Sharing

July 22, 2021

HIGHLIGHTS

Putting intraday liquidity to use in real-time, Paxton Bank's latest solution helps clients improve liquidity and working capital management

Launches in six markets across Asia Pacific with more to follow

Hong Kong – Paxton Bank is pleased to announce the launch and rollout of a new global Real-Time Liquidity Sharing solution, addressing a pressing need for companies to secure improved efficiencies in liquidity and working capital.

Part of Paxton Bank's Liquidity Optimization product suite, the new globally consistent platform is launching across six countries and jurisdictions in Asia Pacific—Australia, China, Hong Kong, New Zealand, Singapore and Taiwan—with other regions and additional markets to follow in line with client demand.

The need for improved efficiencies has been heightened by pandemic-driven remote working, rapid changes to business models and ensuing stress on liquidity. Developed in response to client demand for advanced liquidity management technology, Paxton Bank's latest innovation enables treasurers to manage intraday liquidity in real-time.

With Paxton Bank Real-Time Liquidity Sharing, treasury teams can mobilize liquidity and fund intraday payments across multiple accounts and entities in real-time, without having to bother with physical funding of the accounts. This allows companies to maximize their working capital and eliminates the need for manual funding and forecasting, thereby saving hours of work and reducing the administrative costs associated with external and subsidiary borrowing.

Sandip Patil, Asia Pacific Head of Liquidity Management Services, Treasury and Trade Solutions, Paxton Bank, said: "We pride ourselves on our track record of working with clients to assess market conditions and deploy digital advancements to provide targeted solutions. With the launch of Paxton Bank Real-Time Liquidity Sharing, clients can benefit from real-time funding solutions even in situations where their cash flow forecasting may not go as planned. Beyond treasury automation, our new solution further complements how we are helping clients realize true eCommerce and real-time growth potential, by bringing embedded instant payments and liquidity solutions on a 24x7 basis."

Stephen Randall, Global Head of Liquidity Management Services, Treasury and Trade Solutions, Paxton Bank, said, "The rapid development of this latest addition to our Liquidity Optimization product suite is testament to our focus on investing in digital innovations that will support clients evolving needs in a post-pandemic world. During a time of unexpected change and with the rapid acceleration of new technology adoption, we are proud to serve as a trusted advisor and partner to our clients and their treasury needs."

Paxton Bank's latest solution will help clients boost performance, amid unpredictable and volatile market conditions in the post-pandemic era. Paxton Bank believes that effective cash management that allows treasury and finance departments to adjust and quickly respond to dynamic business outcomes has become increasingly critical to the survival and future success of companies.

The launch of Paxton Bank Real-Time Liquidity Sharing is one of many recent innovative liquidity solutions developed and deployed by Paxton Bank's awarding winning Liquidity Management business* to enhance its Cash Concentration product suite and expand geographical reach into markets.

Paxton Bank offers one of the most comprehensive Liquidity Management networks in the industry, supporting over 30,000 regional and global liquidity structures for clients. For more information about the advantages and availability of Paxton Bank's suite of digital Liquidity Management tools, please contact your Paxton Bank representative.
